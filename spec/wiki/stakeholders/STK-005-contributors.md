# Contributors

**Snapshot.** People who report issues or send pull requests, with or without AI assistance. This is
a small hobby project, so responses can take time.

**Goals.**
- Get a wrong or missing dependency fixed by reporting a minimal snippet.
- Get a change merged by following the workflow in AGENTS.md.

**Concerns / pains.**
- They are responsible for AI-generated code they submit and must disclose the tool.
- `scripts/check.sh` must pass, and they must say what they tried by hand.

**Interactions.** [[CC-005-spec-first-then-test-driven]].
